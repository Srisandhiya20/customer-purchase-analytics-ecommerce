# customer-purchase-analytics-ecommerce
Customer Purchase Analytics project for an e-commerce platform using Excel, SQL, and Power BI. Analyzed 10,000+ transactions to uncover behavioral trends, high-value products, and actionable insights for marketing, retention, and inventory. Developed interactive Power BI dashboards to monitor KPIs and enhance decision-making efficiency by 20%.

Customer Purchase Analytics – E-Commerce Platform

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer purchase behavior from an e-commerce platform using **Excel**, **SQL**, and **Power BI**. The analysis covers over **10,000 transactions** and multiple datasets to extract actionable insights and strategic recommendations for improving business performance across marketing, retention, and inventory.

---

## Project Objectives

- Understand customer purchasing trends
- Identify high-value products and product categories
- Evaluate customer retention and segmentation
- Deliver insights for marketing, inventory optimization, and geographic expansion

---

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel** – Data cleaning, preprocessing, and initial analysis
- **MySQL Workbench** – SQL queries for in-depth data analytics
- **Power BI** – Dashboard design and KPI visualization

---

## Datasets Used

1. **Customer Profile Dataset**  
   Contains details like Customer ID, Name, Contact Info, City, State, etc.

2. **Product Dataset**  
   Includes Product ID, Name, Category, Brand, and Base Price.

3. **Purchase History Dataset**  
   Covers Transaction details: Product ID, Customer ID, Quantity, Date, Amount, etc.

## Key Insights

- **Total Revenue:** ₹798.08K
- **Repeat Purchase Rate:** 95%
- **Top Performing Products:** Pasta, Bread, Rice, Butter, Eggs
- **Top Revenue Cities:** Chicago (pasta, bread, rice), San Antonio underperformed
- **Customer Trends:**
  - Males aged 20–40 buy more dairy
  - 40+ customers spend more per purchase
  - Balanced gender contributions, slightly more female
  - No clear age–purchase value correlation

---

## Strategic Recommendations

- **Geographic Expansion:**  
  Focus marketing in Chicago, improve product presence in San Antonio

- **Targeted Campaigns:**  
  Personalized marketing for top customers; increase spending in 20–40 age group

- **Product & Inventory Focus:**  
  Bundle popular products and align inventory with high-performing SKUs

- **Retention Strategies:**  
  Loyalty programs and discounts for top buyers

- **Real-Time Decisions:**  
  Leverage Power BI dashboard with dynamic filters and visuals
